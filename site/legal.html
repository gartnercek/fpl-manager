Legal

Unofficial, advisory, no password.

A private letter that uses public FPL data. Unofficial. You apply the advice yourself.

Affiliation

FPL Manager is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Premier League or FPL.

What you get now

The first intake is a free beta: emailed advice (transfers, XI, captain, chip timing) produced by the model on How it chooses. You apply changes yourself on the official FPL website. There is no autopilot and no points guarantee. Actual FPL points will differ from expected points.

Paid season (€79 for 2026/27) and monthly (€19) plans are listed on the site as not on sale yet. When they open, we will email the beta list before any card is taken. Joining the beta is not a purchase.

What is stored

If you join the beta: email address, FPL team ID, optional name, and a plan preference (season or monthly) for the later paid rollout. The weekly run then reads the same public FPL endpoints anyone can hit with that ID (squad as of the last deadline, bank, chips, transfer history). No FPL password is requested or stored. No card is collected during the beta.

When paid checkout opens, card numbers will stay with Stripe. Email and team ID may then also be held as Stripe metadata.

Public market prices used in the model are not a bet placed on your behalf.

Questions, a wrong team ID, or a delete request: support@fpl-manager.com

Legal basis (GDPR)

Consent, then contract once you ask for the letter: sending the weekly advice. You may email to correct the team ID, export what is held, or delete the record and stop the letters. Mail is not used for unrelated marketing lists. We may write once when paid plans open; you can refuse.

Payments and cancellation

Prices on the site are in euro and describe the later paid rollout. Checkout is not live. Leave the beta by emailing support. After paid checkout exists: it will be hosted by Stripe at checkout.stripe.com. Monthly would renew until you cancel. A season pass would be a one-time payment for 2026/27 FPL and would not auto-renew into 2027/28. Refunds (once paid): if a letter was not sent for a deadline you paid for, write to support; “Haaland blanked” is not a defect in the goods.

Where VAT applies on a future paid checkout, Stripe Tax may add it. The receipt is the tax invoice.

Limitation

To the extent the law allows, liability for the advice is limited to the fees you paid for the current season, or to zero while the letter is free. Do not stake money you cannot lose on the letter, including on prediction markets.

Site log

The static pages do not set an analytics cookie. The host (Cloudflare Pages) may keep ordinary server logs. Fonts are loaded from Bunny Fonts.
