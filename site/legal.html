Legal

Unofficial, advisory, no password.

A private letter that uses public FPL data. Unofficial. You apply the advice yourself.

Affiliation

FPL Manager is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Premier League or FPL.

What you buy

A subscription is for emailed advice: suggested transfers, XI, captain, and chip timing produced by the model described on How it chooses. You apply changes yourself on the official FPL website. There is no autopilot and no points guarantee. Actual FPL points will differ from expected points.

What is stored

If you subscribe: email address, FPL team ID, chosen plan, and any note you typed, held as Stripe metadata and used by the weekly mailer. The weekly run then reads the same public FPL endpoints anyone can hit with that ID (squad as of the last deadline, bank, chips, transfer history). No FPL password is requested or stored. Card numbers stay with Stripe.

Public market prices used in the model are not a bet placed on your behalf.

Questions, a wrong team ID, or a delete request: support@fpl-manager.com

Legal basis (GDPR)

Contract: sending the letter you asked for. You may email to correct the team ID, export what is held, or delete the record and stop the letters. Mail is not used for unrelated marketing lists.

Payments and cancellation

Prices on the site are in euro. Checkout is hosted by Stripe at checkout.stripe.com. Monthly renews until you cancel — use Manage billing on the thanks page after payment, or the customer portal link on the Stripe receipt. A season pass is a one-time payment for 2026/27 FPL and does not auto-renew into 2027/28. Refunds: if a letter was not sent for a deadline you paid for, write to support; “Haaland blanked” is not a defect in the goods.

Where VAT applies, Stripe Tax may add it at checkout. The receipt is the tax invoice.

Limitation

To the extent the law allows, liability for the advice is limited to the subscription fees you paid for the current season. Do not stake money you cannot lose on the letter, including on prediction markets.

Site log

The static pages do not set an analytics cookie. The host (Cloudflare Pages) may keep ordinary server logs. Fonts are loaded from Bunny Fonts.
