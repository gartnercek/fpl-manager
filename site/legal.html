Legal

Unofficial, advisory, no password.

A private letter that uses public FPL data. Unofficial. You apply the advice yourself.

Affiliation

FPL Manager is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Premier League or FPL.

What you get

Emailed advice (transfers, XI, captain, chip timing) produced by the model on How it chooses. You apply changes yourself on the official FPL website. There is no autopilot and no points guarantee. Actual FPL points will differ from expected points.

Season is a one-time €50 for 2026/27. Monthly is €10 and renews until you cancel. Listed prices include tax where Stripe applies it.

What is stored

Email address, FPL team ID, optional name, and the plan you paid for. The weekly run then reads the same public FPL endpoints anyone can hit with that ID (squad as of the last deadline, bank, chips, transfer history). No FPL password is requested or stored.

Card numbers stay with Stripe. Email and team ID may also be held as Stripe metadata.

Public market prices used in the model are not a bet placed on your behalf.

Questions, a wrong team ID, or a delete request: support@fpl-manager.com

Legal basis (GDPR)

Consent, then contract once you pay: sending the weekly advice. You may email to correct the team ID, export what is held, or delete the record and stop the letters. Mail is not used for unrelated marketing lists.

Payments and cancellation

Prices are in euro and include tax where Stripe applies it. Checkout is hosted by Stripe at checkout.stripe.com. Monthly renews until you cancel from Manage billing or the link on the Stripe receipt. A season pass is a one-time payment for 2026/27 FPL and does not auto-renew into 2027/28. Refunds: if a letter was not sent for a deadline you paid for, write to support; “Haaland blanked” is not a defect in the goods.

Where VAT or sales tax applies, Stripe Managed Payments calculates it and issues the customer receipt. That receipt is the tax invoice for the purchase.

Limitation

To the extent the law allows, liability for the advice is limited to the fees you paid for the current season. Do not stake money you cannot lose on the letter, including on prediction markets.

Site log

The static pages do not set an analytics cookie. The host (Cloudflare Pages) may keep ordinary server logs. Fonts are loaded from Bunny Fonts.
